## TL;DR

Through close analysis of 50 Xiaohongshu posts, Ge and Tian argue that married, child-free women legitimize non-motherhood through explicit age labels, youthful and playful imagery, intimate coupledom, and curated domestic consumption. These representations affirm marriage while challenging compulsory motherhood, but can reproduce beauty standards and class privilege.

Published in *Social Semiotics* 36(3), 319–342 (2026); first published online September 22, 2024. [Article DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/10350330.2024.2405512).

## Research Question

What themes characterize married, child-free discourse on Xiaohongshu, how do textual and visual resources realize them, and how do they relate to broader gender and sociocultural ideologies?

## Motivation

Research on digital motherhood and unmarried “leftover women” leaves married women rejecting reproductive expectations less examined. The authors investigate how these women negotiate motherhood norms within marriage, amid Chinese pronatalist pressures and consumer-oriented ideals of individual responsibility.

## Contributions

- Identifies a form of identity negotiation that maintains marital legitimacy while presenting motherhood as optional.
- Explains how explicit age references combine with youthful imagery and childhood motifs to contest maternal expectations.
- Connects aesthetic homemaking and consumption with entrepreneurial self-governance, while identifying tensions around youth, beauty, and socioeconomic privilege.

## Method

The study uses [[concepts/multimodal-critical-discourse-analysis|Multimodal Critical Discourse Analysis]]. A two-phase search began with the Chinese keyword 已婚无娃, popularity-ranked results, 12 potential influencers, the top 100 posts, and influencer-homepage trending posts. The final snapshot contains 50 high-engagement posts, anonymized A1–A50, including images, text, and occasional film clips. Selection considered three hashtags—married/not yet parenting, recorder of romantic life, and DINK couple—as well as contextually relevant posts without them.

Posts were imported into MAXQDA. Semantic macro-analysis and open and axial coding organized themes; close readings examined lexical choices, nominalization, representations of actors and activities, gaze, spatial boundaries, materials, colors, and legitimation through rationalization and storytelling. The authors report blurring faces and altering names.

## Experiments

This is a qualitative discourse study, not an experiment. Table 1 reports coded theme totals of 85 for exquisite living, 71 for rediscovering childhood and duality, and 52 for deconstructing childbearing-age labels. These exceed the 50-post sample and should not be interpreted as counts of distinct posts or population prevalence.

Sections 6.1–6.2 develop three findings:

- **Age negotiation:** numerical age labels and descriptions of childcare burdens foreground autonomy over time. Youthful imagery accompanies these labels, producing a tension between rejecting maternal age expectations and valuing youthful appearance.
- **Playful coupledom:** toys, games, childlike self-descriptions, and intimate two-person spaces make a child-free marriage appear satisfying and legitimate. The authors interpret practical domestic arrangements and personal narratives as complementary legitimation strategies.
- **Entrepreneurial domesticity:** carefully styled homes, furnishings, brands, and prices present consumption as a route to a desirable autonomous life. Self-management expands from bodily appearance to the curation of an entire lifestyle.

These are the authors' interpretations of representations, not measured effects on fertility decisions or audience attitudes.

## Limitations

The authors emphasize that resisting motherhood norms can reinforce ideals of perpetual youth, thinness, and beauty, and that the depicted lifestyle is particularly accessible to urban middle-class women. Design-based limits include popularity-based selection on one platform, a small snapshot corpus, and the absence of causal or audience-reception evidence. The reported method does not supply intercoder reliability statistics or a precise collection window. Hashtags such as “not yet parenting” also do not independently establish a permanent decision against parenthood.
