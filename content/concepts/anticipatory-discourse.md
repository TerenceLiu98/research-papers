## Overview

Anticipatory discourse expresses expectations, hopes, and concerns about possible futures through present communication. In technology research, it connects imagined innovations to current debates about work, security, governance, and social change.

## Key Ideas

- **Temporal framing:** The reviewed study distinguishes present futures, or projections imagined from the current moment, from future presents, or open-ended possibilities and their implications for present discussion.
- **Anticipatory versus anticipated emotions:** Anticipatory emotions are feelings experienced now about a future event; anticipated emotions are feelings one expects to experience when that event occurs.
- **Computational operationalization:** Topic models map recurring themes, while sentiment and emotion classifiers characterize their affective framing. Positive sentiment and Hope are different constructs; neutral polarity can coexist with future-oriented emotion.
- **Composite scores:** Adding Anticipation to Trust or Fear provides the reviewed study’s Hope or Anxiety proxy. Such scores require construct validation beyond component correlations.
- **Influence versus activity:** Frequent posting identifies active narrators of futures. Establishing effects on public expectations requires evidence of reception or behavioral change.
