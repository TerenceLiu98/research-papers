## Overview

Rumor refutation on social media is the organized production and dissemination of evidence or explanations intended to correct unconfirmed or false claims. RRE is broader than whether one post is persuasive: it can include account authority, content quality, posting timeliness, audience response, and the network position through which corrective information travels.

## Key Ideas

- Refutation depends on both the message and the account distributing it. Authority, originality, clarity, and dedicated debunking activity can affect whether audiences accept and share a correction.
- Timeliness matters because rumors can spread before verification catches up. Audience participation, reading, emotional attitude, and network reach determine how far a correction travels.
- A multi-criteria account evaluation can represent content, subjects, audiences, behaviors, and network context together rather than reducing RRE to reposts, likes, or comments.
- Corrections should be adapted to public concerns and supported by credible evidence. The paper's keyword analysis emphasizes practical health and safety information and official or scientific responses during major events.
- Platform-specific indicators create transfer risks: a Weibo-based measure of followers, verification, repost layers, or big-V followers needs reinterpretation elsewhere.
