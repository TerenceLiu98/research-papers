## Overview

Cross-border information flows connect national information environments through media organizations, institutions, and individual users. Observing similar content in two countries is a starting point for detecting transmission, but does not itself identify where that information originated or how it traveled.

## Key Ideas

- **Origin differs from posting order.** Domestic discussion may precede a viral foreign post while still concern an event originating abroad.
- **Transmission differs from agreement.** A rebuttal can introduce the foreign claim it criticizes to a domestic audience.
- **Platforms are observation sites.** Matching posts does not establish that information traveled directly between those platforms; television, websites, or other intermediaries may connect them.
- **Gatekeeping can be plural.** State-controlled and commercialized media, unaffiliated users, and foreign organizations can all transmit information under censorship.
- **Denominators matter.** The share of all sampled posts with inflow differs from the share after removing domestically originating or directionally ambiguous cases.
