## Overview

Neural simulation-based inference learns inferential quantities from parameter-output pairs generated by a simulator when evaluating its likelihood is impractical. Neural posterior estimation fits a conditional parameter density, neural likelihood estimation fits the simulator likelihood, and neural ratio estimation learns a likelihood-to-evidence ratio. These approaches can separate the expensive production of training simulations from subsequent inference.

## Key Ideas

- **Posterior learning:** Given $\theta\sim p(\theta)$ and $x\sim p(x\mid\theta)$, maximize $\log q_\phi(\theta\mid x)$. A conditional normalizing flow supports density evaluation and direct sampling.
- **Likelihood learning:** Fit $q_\phi(x\mid\theta)$ on prior-predictive pairs, then combine it with a prior and sample the posterior with MCMC. A per-observation likelihood can be composed across exchangeable observations and reused with a different prior, but approximation errors can accumulate as the dataset grows.
- **Ratio learning:** Distinguishing samples from $p(x,\theta)$ and $p(x)p(\theta)$ yields $r(x,\theta)=p(\theta\mid x)/p(\theta)$. The approximate posterior is proportional to the learned ratio times the prior; it requires a further sampling procedure.
- **Amortization:** Training across the prior predictive distribution makes one estimator reusable for multiple observations. This is distinct from sequential training focused on a particular observation.
- **Sequential proposals:** Later rounds simulate in promising parameter regions. Posterior-estimation objectives must account for the proposal change to avoid learning the wrong posterior.
- **Learned summaries:** Recurrent or graph embedding networks can summarize structured simulator output jointly with the inference objective. Their architecture and information retention still matter.
- **Validation:** Full-distribution comparisons, posterior predictive checks, and calibration examine different aspects of inference. Small point-estimation error alone says little about posterior uncertainty.

## Important Papers

- [[papers/flow-matching-for-scalable-simulation-based-inference|Flow Matching for Scalable Simulation-Based Inference]]: introduces a continuous-flow posterior estimator with flexible conditioning architectures, tractable densities, and experiments on standard SBI tasks and GW150914.
- [[papers/black-box-bayesian-inference-for-agent-based-models|Black-box Bayesian inference for agent-based models]]: benchmarks posterior and ratio estimation for economic simulators and demonstrates posterior learning from graph sequences.
- [[papers/conditional-mean-flows-few-step-densities-for-simulation-based-inference|Conditional Mean Flows: Few-Step Densities for Simulation-Based Inference]]: develops few-step conditional posterior and likelihood densities, including masked marginal likelihoods and repeated evaluation within MCMC.
- Greenberg, Nonnenmacher, and Macke (2019), "Automatic posterior transformation for likelihood-free inference": sequential posterior learning.
- Hermans, Begy, and Louppe (2020), "Likelihood-free MCMC with amortized approximate ratio estimators": amortized ratio estimation for posterior sampling.
