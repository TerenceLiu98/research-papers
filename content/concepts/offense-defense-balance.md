## Overview

The relative ease or cost of attacking compared with defending in a military environment.

## Key Ideas

Systemic measures classify historical periods, whereas dyadic measures characterize particular opponents. These scopes should not be conflated. Weapons usable for both attack and counterattack make classification contestable.

Offensive tactical mobility does not by itself establish that an entire war will end quickly: counteroffensives and strategic depth can prolong fighting. Nilsson’s historical duration analysis finds no association significant at .05 across four systemic codings.
