## Overview

Creative output homogeneity describes a population producing a narrow range of ideas in response to creative tasks. An answer can be distant from its prompt yet close to other respondents' answers, so individual originality does not guarantee collective diversity.

## Key Ideas

- Assess originality within responses separately from semantic diversity between respondents.
- Compare responses to shared stimuli and account for formatting, lexical overlap, and model-family composition.
- Increased sampling randomness can increase semantic distance through incoherence; diversity must be interpreted alongside appropriateness.
- Similarity across independent outputs does not by itself establish a causal effect on human collaborators or identify a shared internal mechanism.
