## Overview

Relational labour makes ongoing social connection part of economic work. In creative industries, audience contact, responsiveness and strategic self-disclosure can sustain recognition and income while demanding time and emotional effort.

## Key Ideas

- Relationships require maintenance beyond the production of a creative artefact; interaction can also shape what gets produced.
- Emotional investment and economic returns need not accrue directly or equally. Audience trust may support income obtained from advertisers.
- Lu’s triadic extension links creators, audiences and brands under platform mediation. Platforms structure visibility and monetisation rather than acting as neutral conduits.
- Maintenance can coexist with tactical resistance, such as declining sponsorships or refusing hostile interaction. Temporary relief does not establish improved bargaining power.

## Important Papers

- Baym (2015), “Connect with your audience! The relational labor of connection”: develops the concept through musicians’ ongoing audience relationships, as discussed by Lu.
- [[papers/between-audience-lords-and-rich-fathers|Between ‘audience lords’ and ‘rich fathers’]]: studies triadic relational labour through fifteen interviews with Chinese technology creators.
