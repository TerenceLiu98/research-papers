## Overview

Pseudo-relational accommodation (PRA) is the unilateral cognitive, emotional, and behavioral work a user performs to compensate for a relational AI's inability to intentionally reciprocate or participate in mutual repair. The term distinguishes apparent relational adaptation from the reciprocal adjustment assumed in interpersonal relationships.

## Key Ideas

- Restorative accommodation seeks to recover prior interaction through diagnostic probing, system calibration, or relationship retraining.
- Adaptive accommodation renegotiates the bond through relationship downgrade, agent diversification, strategic de-anthropomorphism, or anthropomorphic parallelism.
- Endurant accommodation normalizes recurring breakdowns and preserves interaction through passive acceptance.
- PRA can lead toward relational detachment when failures are treated as persistent technical constraints, or toward relational reaffirmation when AI shortcomings are reframed as tolerable human-like flaws.
- [[Algorithmic Relational Fracture]] supplies the attributional sensemaking that precedes and shapes these responses in [[Relational AI]] relationships.
