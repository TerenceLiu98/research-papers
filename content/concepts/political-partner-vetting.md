## Overview

Seeking or assessing political information about prospective romantic partners to evaluate compatibility. Political partner-vetting is an information-seeking practice that can support political homophily. It is distinct from similarity between actual partners and from changes in political attitudes after a relationship forms.

## Key Ideas

Reading political cues in profiles is a passive screening strategy. Stated importance of such cues is a proxy, not a behavioral count. Its emotional associations can differ by relationship goals: Chen and Mou report positive associations with excitement among non-love seekers but worse emotional outcomes among long-term relationship seekers.

## Important Papers

- [[papers/trusting-dating-algorithms-in-love-and-politics|Chen and Mou (2025)]] examines how algorithmic belief, political screening, and emotional experience covary across three dating-motivation groups in a cross-sectional survey.
