## Overview

Common-pool resource dilemmas concern rival use of a shared stock. Extraction rewards individuals immediately but can undermine future returns for everyone. Sustainable governance requires coordinating use with regeneration and maintaining compliance with agreed limits.

## Key Ideas

- A collective sustainability bound differs from an individual's share of that bound.
- Communication can support agreements, but agreements alone do not guarantee restraint or robustness to newcomers.
- Universalization reasoning asks what would happen if everyone adopted a proposed action; it can make collective consequences salient.
- Resource survival, productive use, and equality measure different outcomes. A community can preserve resources while underusing them or distributing gains unequally.
