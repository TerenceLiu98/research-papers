## Overview

Beliefs about the capacity of matchmaking algorithms to identify compatible romantic partners. These beliefs concern perceived capability, not measured prediction accuracy. They can shape expectations about matches and the extent to which users undertake their own screening.

## Key Ideas

Trust need not imply passive acceptance. Chen and Mou find opposite belief–screening associations across motivation groups. Positive direct emotional associations can coexist with negative indirect associations. Their single item about predicting eventual love is narrower than a multi-item trust scale.

## Important Papers

- [[papers/trusting-dating-algorithms-in-love-and-politics|Chen and Mou (2025)]] examines how algorithmic belief, political screening, and emotional experience covary across three dating-motivation groups in a cross-sectional survey.
