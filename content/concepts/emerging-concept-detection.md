## Overview

Emerging concept detection identifies semantic content that becomes newly prominent relative to earlier discourse. Sentence-level representations allow concepts to span multiple lexical expressions.

## Key Ideas

- Temporal contrast separates new semantic density from established topics.
- Persistence requirements distinguish sustained emergence from transient fads; longer linking gaps can capture rediscovery.
- Embeddings and dimensionality reduction determine which semantic distinctions remain visible. Density comparisons require shared coordinates.
- A historical event may yield multiple concepts. Selected-event recovery does not establish exhaustive precision or recall.
- A projection fitted on the whole corpus enables retrospective comparison but does not itself demonstrate prospective detection.
