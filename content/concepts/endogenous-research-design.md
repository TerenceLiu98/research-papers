## Overview

Research design is endogenous when analytical choices are themselves shaped by researcher characteristics, preferences, or responses to the data. Variable construction, sample selection, and model specification can then be part of the process connecting a researcher's prior commitments to the findings they report. Holding the initial data and hypothesis constant does not hold this decision process constant.

## Key Ideas

- **Confounders and mediators serve different roles.** Disciplinary training may influence both policy preferences and analytical practice. A chosen specification may instead lie on a proposed pathway from preferences to findings. Conditioning on both indiscriminately can obscure the association one intends to study; causal interpretations still require justified assumptions.
- **Combinations can matter more than individual choices.** Outcome construction, sample inclusion, exposure measurement, and model structure can interact. Weak associations for individual decisions do not imply that the joint design is unimportant.
- **A design-based decomposition is descriptive.** Grouping models by specification and comparing their average estimates can show which choices accompany differences in findings. It does not alone establish that preferences caused those choices or identify a mediated causal effect.
- **Observed final models do not reveal the search process.** Without records of discarded specifications and stopping decisions, a preference-result association cannot establish deliberate result selection or distinguish it from unconscious judgment.
- **Peer assessment is not ground truth.** A design's conformity with reviewers' expectations and its ability to recover the true parameter are distinct properties.

## Important Papers

- [[papers/ideological-bias-in-the-production-of-research-findings|Ideological bias in the production of research findings]]: Borjas and Breznau (2026) associate immigration preferences with estimates from 71 research teams. Five dimensions of specification choice descriptively account for 68% of the adjusted pro- versus anti-immigration team gap, while the observational ideology measure limits causal interpretation.
