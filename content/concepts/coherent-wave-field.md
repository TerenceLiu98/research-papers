## Overview

For a fixed source, each realization of a random medium produces a different field. Averaging displacement at the same position and time preserves contributions with a reproducible phase. Fluctuations around that mean can still carry energy even when their signed average vanishes.

## Key Ideas

- Scattering can attenuate the coherent field even when the material absorbs no energy. A growing share of the transmitted energy can reside in fluctuations around the mean.
- Multiply scattered waves can contribute to the coherent signal. Several modes can survive averaging and appear as distinct ridges when the field is resolved in frequency and wavenumber.

## Important Papers

[[papers/multiple-propagating-modes-in-random-polycrystals|Multiple Propagating Modes in Random Polycrystals]] tracks an incident pulse as its wavefront breaks up across individual grains. The averaged displacement at greater depths resolves two pulses of the original polarization.
