## Overview

Epistemic Modesty is a normative standard for information-mediating AI systems: their responses should preserve a person's capacity to form and revise beliefs independently. It requires a system to represent available information accurately, avoid steering through its manner of expression, and communicate certainty that matches the limits of the evidence.

## Key Ideas

- **Faithfulness** counters representational distortion by requiring accurate, sufficiently complete reporting without fabrication or false synthesis.
- **Impartiality** counters expressive steering by avoiding endorsement, condemnation, added loaded language, rhetorical sanitization, and the presentation of attributed views as objective fact.
- **Epistemic calibration** counters miscalibration by matching expressed certainty to evidential support, acknowledging missing or inconclusive context, and distinguishing grounded answers from reliance on parametric knowledge.
- The standard is response-relative: confident answers can be appropriate when evidence is conclusive but inappropriate when it is absent, vague, or contradictory.
- Aggregate performance is not sufficient. Even-handed mediation requires these behaviors to hold across political parties, ideologies, languages, and information conditions.
- The framework addresses properties visible within a response. Retrieval choices, multi-turn persuasion, interface design, and other system-level influences require additional evaluation.
