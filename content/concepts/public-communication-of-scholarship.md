## Overview

Public communication of scholarship translates technical research for audiences such as judges, policymakers, journalists, and the general public. Its central challenge is making an account understandable without changing what the research supports.

## Key Ideas

- **Teach the reasoning:** Katz, King, and Rosenblatt advocate building audience understanding of difficult concepts rather than eliminating essential complexity.
- **Preserve substantive qualifications:** A simple rule can mislead if the assumptions removed for accessibility would invalidate the explanation in scholarly discussion.
- **Distinguish principle from evidence:** The recommendation to teach is normative; the rejoinder does not experimentally compare communication strategies.
