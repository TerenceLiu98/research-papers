## Overview

The process through which far-right ideas, discourses, or policies become legitimised and incorporated into mainstream political life.

## Key Ideas

Mainstream actors can actively produce exclusionary agendas, rather than simply borrowing them under electoral pressure. Normalisation therefore need not track far-right parties’ vote shares or participation in government. Analysis should distinguish organisational inclusion from the legitimacy and policy reach of ideas.

## Important Papers

[[papers/the-pretence-of-the-cordon-sanitaire-non-collaboration-as-a-distraction-from-discursive-congruence|Shuttleworth, Brown, and Mondon (2025)]] compares France, Germany, and the UK to show how formal non-collaboration can coexist with discursive congruence. The paper cautions against assuming an inherently oppositional relationship between mainstream and far-right politics.
