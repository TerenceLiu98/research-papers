## Overview

Survey interview quality control evaluates whether collected interviews follow fieldwork protocols closely enough to retain. It covers both fabrication and genuine interviews compromised by skipped questions, incorrect administration, or sampling violations.

## Key Ideas

- Combine real-time flags, batch metadata checks, and human review of audio or other records; these sources reveal different failures.
- Examine individual interviews, enumerator behavior, sampling clusters, and sample-wide duplication rather than relying on a single level.
- Early detection supports correction and replacement while enumerators remain in the field.
- Institutional acceptance decisions are useful training labels but imperfect proxies for underlying quality.
- Predictive importance is conditional on other checks and does not itself measure implementation cost or causal effects on survey error.
